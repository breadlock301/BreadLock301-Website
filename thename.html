When I first, uh, "was" (it's a long story,) I didn't have my own name. I was just. Catgirl. At first, I was considered for the name "Flandre" (YES I'M SERIOUS,) but after some consideration, I decided to go with Bread instead, a name I gave to a cat that I used to own. He ran away one day when we moved though 3: and while using a name that I gave to a male cat for me is weird in context, I don't normally give the context, so it's totally fine! :D

This stuck for a while, but then I entered... THE WORLD!!! And nobody's gonna call someone by a name that sounds like "bred", so what did I do?

...With all the Neocities shadowbanning going around lately, I don't know if I could tell you what I went with. But I can certainly tell you that it wasn't any better.

Anyways, after spending a lot of time trying to come up with an actual name, in around 2024 or so I settled on Breadette, which is just "Bread" but with "-ette" suffixed onto it. And y'know what? It was good for the time. But as time went on, I became disatisfied with it. It was still derivative of the word "Bread", and it really, really sounded too much like someone trying too hard (or perhaps not trying hard enough?) to make a unique name, so I went back to the drawing board. After some Wiktionaring, I came up with Brenna, based on Brennan, which itself is derived from the welsh word for Crow. I like corvids.

Aya.
