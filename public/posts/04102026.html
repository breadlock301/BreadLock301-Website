Duolingo Progress

Plenty! I'm currently on Section 17 of Unit 2. After watching "Why Can't Kids Read Anymore?" by ColdFusion and reading the comments, I had a realisation that writing stuff down is probably a good thing.

Okay okay wait I know that's vague but. The thought proccess is that because writing stuff down requires a lot of mental... what'stheword... anyways, since it's a skill that activates a variety of areas in your brain, it should be helpful in memorising all of the vocabulary Duolingo throws at me more quickly, which would let me get through sections faster. And while I've only done this for a few days now, I can feel an improvement. Writing stuff down helps a lot with the short term memory of how to spell and use certain words, and makes retaining those words more natural. Plus, if I get stuck on something, I can just look at the previous sentences I've written down. Oh yeah, I should probably clarify. I'm writing EVERY Swedish phrase/sentence from every question. Nyo English translation, nyo singular words (though I've also tried writing original sentences when new words appear in isolation, which should also help with sentence building since that's also important to develope,) and I just. Fill each page in this almost completley empty notebook I had lying around with these Swedish sentences/phrases. But yee, as I was saying, it's been really helpful in making what feels like meaningful progress. Oh yeah it also also makes me take more time on lessons, and because of the extra interactivity of writing the sentences down, it helps absorb stuff. Yeah point is it's good. Ja!
